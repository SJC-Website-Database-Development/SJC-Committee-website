Please join us for our annual Traditional Christmas Tea, Sunday Dec 6th, 1-3pm. Gently used jewelry, scarves and purses will be for sale. You could win a door prize. Admission is $15 and the event will take place in the St Joseph Church Hall in the basement of the Church.
